## Part 4 — Documents Ethiopians Usually Need

1. Passport (valid ≥ 18 months)
2. Degree certificate + transcript (**MoE / ETA authentication** for international use)
3. English test: IELTS / TOEFL / Duolingo (British Council & IDP Addis Ababa)
4. CV (Europass for Europe) → *use ScholarAI CV Maker*
5. Motivation letter / Statement of Purpose
6. 2–3 Recommendation letters
7. Research proposal (PhD)
8. Medical certificate + police clearance (some countries)
9. Certified translations (if documents are in Amharic)
